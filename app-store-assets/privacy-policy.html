Privacy Policy - Just an Agenda

Effective Date: September 20, 2026

🔒 Privacy-First App

YOUR TASKS STAY ON YOUR DEVICE.

No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. The only time anything leaves your device is if you choose to send a bug report.

Summary

"Just an Agenda" is a local-first agenda. We (the developer) do not operate servers that store your tasks, and the app does not collect analytics or usage data. The sections below describe exactly what is stored, what can be sent, and to whom.

Information Stored on Your Device

  • Your tasks and notes: the text, dates, completion state, repeat rules and reminder times you enter.
  • Your settings: language, theme, font size and page layout preferences.

This information is kept in the app's private storage on your device and is never uploaded by the app to us or to anyone else. It stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ No accounts: there is no sign-up or login, and we do not ask for your name or email to use the app.
  • ❌ No analytics or tracking: the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking libraries.
  • ❌ No crash-reporting tools: the app does not include a crash-reporting service.
  • ❌ No location, contacts or device calendar: the app does not request access to your location, your contacts or your device's calendar. The calendar screen inside the app only shows the tasks you created in Just an Agenda.

When Information Leaves Your Device

The app works fully offline. It uses the internet only in these situations, both started by you:

1. Sending a bug report (optional)

If you use Settings → Report a bug and tap send, the app submits the following to a third-party form-delivery service, Web3Forms (web3forms.com), which forwards it to the developer's email inbox:

  • The description you wrote.
  • The email address you typed, only if you chose to provide one (so we can reply to you).
  • The app version, your operating system name and version, and the date.

Like any web service, Web3Forms also receives technical data that accompanies an internet request, such as your IP address. Its handling of that data is described in Web3Forms' own privacy policy. If sending fails, the app offers to open your email app with the same text prefilled instead; nothing is sent until you send that email yourself. Your tasks are never included in a bug report.

2. Opening links

Links in the app (for example this policy, the developer's website, or web addresses you write inside a task) open in your device's browser when you tap them. Those websites are governed by their own privacy policies.

Permissions the App Uses

  • Notifications: to show the reminders you set.
  • Exact alarms (Android): so a reminder is delivered at the time you chose. If you do not allow it, reminders may arrive a little late.
  • Vibration and start-up (Android): for reminder alerts, and to restore your scheduled reminders after the device restarts.
  • Internet: only for the optional bug report and for opening links described above.

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device by its operating system; they do not pass through our servers. You can change notification permissions at any time in your device settings.

Data Security

  • Device protection: your data is protected by your device's built-in security (screen lock and operating-system storage protection, which may include encryption depending on your device and settings).
  • No extra encryption layer: the app does not add its own encryption on top of what your device provides, so we recommend using a screen lock.
  • No transmission of tasks: the app does not send your tasks anywhere.

Device Backups

Your operating system may include the app's data in your own device backup (for example Google backup on Android, or iCloud / computer backups on iPhone) if you have backups enabled. Those backups are created and controlled by Google or Apple under their own policies and are tied to your account; we do not have access to them. This means your tasks may be restored if you reinstall the app or set up a new device with a backup.

Third-Party Services

  • Web3Forms: delivers optional bug reports to the developer, as described above.
  • Google Play / Apple App Store: we distribute the app through these stores. They may independently provide developers with aggregated diagnostic or usage statistics from users who opted in to share them with the store. That is governed by their policies, not by any tool in our app.
  • Your device's notification system: delivers your local reminders.

Your Rights and Control

  • Access and deletion: your tasks are on your device; you can edit or delete them at any time in the app.
  • Uninstalling: removes the app's local data from your device. Copies inside your own device backups (see above) are managed through your Google or Apple account.
  • Bug reports: if you sent a bug report and want it deleted, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.
  • Notifications: manage or revoke permission in your device settings.

Data Retention

  • Tasks and settings: kept on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • Bug reports: kept in the developer's email only as long as needed to investigate and reply, and deleted on request.
  • No server copies of your tasks: we do not have backups of your tasks anywhere.

Children's Privacy

Just an Agenda is a general-audience app. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, other than what a user may choose to write in an optional bug report. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
